Data completeness & governance

*Counts across viruses are pathogen-specific test results and must not be interpreted as unique individuals or unique specimens. Influenza A and B are grouped into total influenza to avoid subtype double-counting. Influenza A(H1N1)pdm09, A(H3N2), unsubtyped A, B, and combined A may overlap if selected together; these must not be added as unique infections. Subtype positivity uses all influenza tests as denominator. Province/territory codes marked * are published laboratory aggregates that may not include all reporting facilities, so interprovincial comparisons are provisional. Historical reporting, test eligibility, laboratory participation and revisions differ. COVID-19 data are absent from the earlier seasons and should not be imputed as zero. PHAC warns against naive comparisons over time and geography.
